**Paint, 1945.** Japanese merchant: light grey-green over hull, superstructure, masts and funnel, diagonal or wavy dark pine-green
bands (sometimes Kure grey); decks red-brown primer or dark grey-green, Type 2A hatch covers weathered tan wood; dull red-brown
antifouling below the waterline under a thin black boot-topping; names and funnel bands painted out. US: Liberty overall haze grey
(Measure 13), deck blue decks, wide black boot-topping over antifouling red; T2 Navy Blue up to the main deck edge and haze grey above
(Measure 22, Navy oilers) or overall haze grey (Measure 13), deck blue decks and catwalk, black boot-topping over red.

## Ground crews

- **USAAF:** herringbone-twill two-piece fatigues or one-piece coveralls, light sage green or the darker olive drab #7 (most by 1945),
  sleeves rolled or an OD undershirt; A-3 mechanic's cap (visor flipped up) or the "Daisy Mae" hat; tan roughout boots or M-1943 buckle boots.
- **Japanese army (IJAAF):** khaki-tan or light sage cotton coveralls or two-piece fatigues; khaki-brown field cap with a yellow star
  and neck flaps; dark split-toe tabi or ankle boots with puttees.
- **Japanese navy (IJNAS):** blue-grey or dark navy two-piece work dress or coveralls, yellow anchor patch; in the heat blue-grey
  trousers, white sleeveless undershirt and white headband.

## Airfields

| Feature | Japanese | American |
|---|---|---|
| Runway | Rolled crushed coral or packed earth, ~1,200-1,800 x 45-60 | Crushed white coral, asphalt or Marston mat, ~2,100-2,600 x 60 (B-29); fighter strips PSP |
| Revetments | U-shaped earth/coral berms 15-20 wide, coconut-log or sandbag faced, palm-frond nets; concrete arch shelters at rear bases | Open coral hardstands, low 2-3 m bulldozed blast walls |
| Control tower | Low concrete bunker or camouflaged timber frame, 2-3 storeys, 7-10 m, flat roof with a sandbagged AA position | Timber or bolted steel-angle, 3-4 storeys, 12-15 m, angled green-tinted glass cab, catwalk, windsock mast; beside a Quonset ops hut |
| Buildings | Dispersed wooden barracks, low corrugated-iron or timber sheds under nets, earth-covered concrete magazines, slit trenches | Quonset huts on concrete slabs, boardwalks, power poles, open timber maintenance sheds with A-frames |
| Fuel | Buried concrete cisterns, stacks of 200 L drums in the tree line | Rubberised bladders in earth dikes, bolted steel tank farms |
| AA | Type 96 25 mm single, twin and triple in sandbag pits; Type 98 20 mm; Type 88 75 mm in 4-6 gun batteries; 150 cm searchlights with sound locators | 40 mm Bofors in sandbag pits, M45 quad .50, 90 mm batteries with SCR-584 radar; 60 in searchlights |

**US structures in detail**
- Quonset hut, Stran-Steel 20 x 48 ft: 6.10 x 14.63 m, 3.15 m high outside; 13 ribs at 1.22 m (12 bays); corrugated steel, 3 sheets
  round the arc; flat end walls with a central double door 1.12 x 2.03 m, two windows, gable vent. T-rib 16 x 36 ft: 4.88 x 10.97 m.
  Elephant 40 x 100 ft: 12.19 x 30.48 m, 6.10 m high, 26 ribs, big sliding end doors.
- Butler combat hangar: 39.62 m clear span x 48.77 m long x 11.89 m crown; 9 or 11 three-hinged lattice box-truss arches at 4.88-6.10 m;
  corrugated sheeting (lower side sheets sometimes left off); olive-drab canvas curtain doors under a box-girder header; often a tail notch
  4.5-6 m deep in the back wall.
- Marston mat (M8 PSP): plank 3.05 x 0.381 m (half planks 1.52 m), laid in brick bond; two stiffening ribs; 87 holes of 60 mm in three rows.

| Feature | Japanese | American |
|---|---|---|
| Piers | Coral-block moles, timber T-piers, stone quays 6-10 m wide over shallow shelves; deep-draught ships anchored offshore and worked by Daihatsu barges and wooden lighters | Seabee NL pontoon causeways (5 x 7 x 5 ft boxes: 3x12 = 21 x 175 ft barges, 4x18 = 28 x 260 ft piers), sheet-pile wharves with timber decks |

| Harbour defence | Wire ring nets on logs or drums; controlled minefields; 14-15 cm guns in concrete casemates, 12.7 cm twin and 12 cm in barbettes, 8 cm and 75 mm in waterline caves | Steel ring nets on 5 ft buoys, net-layers at the gate; 155 mm on Panama mounts, 5 in and 90 mm pedestal guns |
| Pickets | Wooden subchasers, armed trawlers (in this game: the Ukuru and the T-14 stand for them) | SC and PC subchasers, picket boats, PT boats |

from the pier head into hillside caves.
