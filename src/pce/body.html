USS PCE-842

The patrol craft escort this game is built around · · Alpha
Alpha: this game is an early work in progress. Expect missing features, rough edges and changes between versions.

The ship

PCE-842 was built by the Pullman-Standard Car Manufacturing Company in Chicago: laid down on 12 June 1943, launched on 14 November 1943 and commissioned on 29 January 1944 at New Orleans. She was the first of the PCE-842 class, the only ships the US Navy ever designated “patrol craft escort”: 68 were built for the US Navy and 17 more went to the Allies under Lend-Lease. The design was an escort version of the 180 ft hull of the Admirable-class minesweepers, a cheaper stand-in for destroyers and destroyer escorts on coastal and convoy escort work.

She was 184 ft 6 in (56.24 m) long with a 33 ft 1 in (10.08 m) beam, with two General Motors diesels driving two shafts for about 15.7 knots, and a crew of about 99. She carried a 3″/50 gun forward, twin 40 mm Bofors and 20 mm Oerlikons, a Hedgehog, four K-gun depth-charge projectors and two stern depth-charge racks.

Her war and after

In 1944 she escorted convoys between Trinidad and Recife. In January 1945 she sailed by way of Panama to Hollandia, then by the Palaus to the Philippine Sea Frontier, where she patrolled against submarines and escorted shipping round the Philippines until the war ended. The game is set there, in 1945.

She was decommissioned at New Orleans on 7 November 1947 and served the Naval Reserve, was named USS Marfa in 1956, and in 1961 went to the Republic of Korea Navy as ROKS Dangpo. She was sunk by North Korean coastal artillery on 19 January 1967.

CONTROLS

Keyboard and mouse, and game controller. The Controls box on screen (H) shows only what you need where you are.

Keyboard & mouse

Game controller

Phone & tablet

FullscreenThe flight starts fullscreen, which hides the Android status and navigation bars. Swipe in from the screen edge to show them for a moment. The ⛶ button toggles it, and the pause menu can turn the automatic fullscreen off.
ExitTap the pause button, then Exit game. It leaves fullscreen and closes the app. This works even when the phone’s swipe and Back gestures are hidden by fullscreen.
Hold itSideways (landscape) like a steering wheel works best; portrait works too. Keep the screen upright and facing you: leaning it back a long way makes a twist also nudge the roll. The game calibrates to the way you hold the phone when you start; the target button in the top bar calibrates again.
RollTurn the phone like a steering wheel: clockwise rolls right.
PitchTop edge toward you pulls up; top edge away pushes the nose down (settings can invert it).
RudderTwist the phone left or right, as if turning on the spot. The twist fades after a few seconds, so use the two arrow buttons at the bottom for sustained rudder, for example on the takeoff roll.
ThrottleDrag the slider on the left; drag it to the very top or bottom to snap to full or idle.
ButtonsFIRE (hold), rocket, bomb, gear, brake (hold), flaps up and down. The top bar has the mission menu, pause menu, camera, calibrate, tilt settings and fullscreen.
Without tiltDrag on the right half of the screen as a virtual stick (drag down to pull up). Pinch zooms the camera in or out: it always stays directly behind the plane and never rotates. Double-tap resets the zoom. The camera button switches to the cockpit view.
GraphicsPause menu > Graphics: Auto, Low, Medium or High (N cycles them). Auto picks Low or High and adjusts the resolution to keep the frame rate smooth. Low draws fewer, simpler trees and fewer clouds and no shadow; Medium is in between; High draws everything at full resolution, with detailed branching trees (several kinds of oak and conifer, with textured leaves and needles) around you; to see them, fly to the wooded hill about 4.6 km north-west of the start (heading 323). Close to your own aircraft (Medium and High) it also shows engraved panel lines, rivets, gun barrels, a pitot tube, fuel caps and wheel detail. Near the ground (Medium and High) blade tufts and wild flowers grow around you, fading out about 130 m away. Auto shrinks the zone of detailed trees and grass first, then the resolution, if the frame rate drops. The choice is saved.
Install / offlineWhen the game is served from a web address (for example GitHub Pages) the browser can install it as an app (Add to Home Screen or Install) and it then runs full screen and offline.
Hardcore modePause menu > Hardcore: a master switch and a switch for each real-aircraft detail (torque, spins, altitude, engine temperature, fuel, war emergency power, real-aircraft performance, load limits, trim, real flap and gear speeds, engine power limits, wind, turbulence, wet runway, icing). Off by default.
LayoutSwipe any instrument box off the screen to hide it. The pause menu has a checkbox for everything you can show or hide, and Move boxes lets you drag the boxes and buttons where you want them. Your choices are remembered, separately for landscape and portrait.
TipsiPhone and iPad ask permission for motion sensors the first time. If the page is shown inside another page the browser may block the sensors: open it in its own tab. Use Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app.

HARDCORE SIMULATION

The real ship, for sailors who want it. Off by default: the game is fun first.

Hardcore mode adds what the real PCE-842 and her crew had to deal with and the game mode leaves out. Switch it on here, then keep only the parts you enjoy: each part has its own switch, so you can, for example, set your own 3″/50 range but keep the aiming rings. With the master switch off, none of them apply.

What it changes

Ship handlingThe bells must be rung from the engine room or the pilothouse; the sea moves her at every station.
GunneryYour own range, your own lead, the real hit rates, the real loading times, smoke in the way.
AntisubmarineYou walk to each weapon, arm it yourself, and have 4 seconds to carry out each order. A shallow charge can damage her.

OPERATIONS

The stations, practice, gunnery, the torpedoman's work and the ship

Missions (the Missions tab, or press M)

Free patrolThe ship is yours: conn her from the pilothouse, answer the bells, keep watch. No targets out.
Gunnery practiceOne for each gun (the 3″/50, the 20 mm and the twin 40 mm): puts out the practice targets and takes you to that gun.
Antisubmarine attack runTakes you to the torpedoman's station with the exercise submarine out. The OOD runs in on him and phones the orders; you fire the Hedgehog, the K-guns and the racks. One run, then the debrief and the pattern plot stay up until you press T again or leave the station.
Sonar watchThe communications station with the exercise submarine out: find him on the sonar and the radar.

The stations (keys 1 to 5; press again for the next view there)

1 OODThe pilothouse: conn the ship (W / S rings the engines, A / D the rudder) at the wheel, the telegraphs and the compass; X for the binoculars.
2 EngineeringThe forward engine room: the telegraph rings, the red needle is the order and the black one the answer; the gauges follow the engines.
3 CommunicationsRadar, sonar and radio (press 3 again for each set). Radar: lay the bearing cursor (Q / E) and range strobe (R / F) on a contact and report it (Space); two reports 40 s apart give its course and speed. Sonar: search with the beam (Q / E), take the cuts (Z / X), call the Doppler (U / B / N) and report (Space): the OOD attacks on your reports, so lose the contact and the attack fails. Radio: acknowledge (Space) and route to the bridge (B). Away from the station an AI operator does it all. T here: the Comms school for the set you are at.
4 GunnerThe 3″/50 forward, the 20 mm amidships and the twin 40 mm aft (press 4 again for each). The mouse lays the gun; hold the backtick (`) to move it six times faster.
5 TorpedomanThe Hedgehog, the port and starboard K-guns and the stern racks. You cannot steer from here: the OOD cons the ship.
C chase viewThe camera outside the ship; C again returns to your station.

Gunnery

FireLeft mouse button, Space or the controller trigger.
Aiming ringsGame mode: a ring on each target shows where to lay the sight so the shells meet it; out of the gun's range there is no aiming ring and the target's own ring turns grey.
3″/50 rangeGame mode: the sight-setter ranges the target nearest your sight. Hardcore: you set it (mouse wheel, Page Up / Page Down).
ReloadingGuns reload by themselves when empty; R reloads on demand. Changing to a gun finds it loaded.
Practice targets (T)Rafts out to 6,000 yards (two inside 20 mm range), sleeves towed by two aircraft (one for the 40 mm, one passing close for the 20 mm), PT-108 towing a target sled at 30 knots, and the exercise submarine. A shot sleeve streams again after 25 seconds. Hitting PT-108 brings “check fire”.
Air raidsSettings > Enable enemy: Planes (or All). Every few minutes two Ki-84 Hayates come out from the enemy airfield; the lookout calls them on the radio. Each makes three strafing passes (a 20–30° dive, firing inside about 1,000 yd, pulling out over the ship), then goes home. Man the guns (5) and shoot them down; hits on the ship are counted. Kill rates follow the Hardcore setting, as for the sleeves.
Shore batteriesSettings > Enable enemy: Shore (or All). Each Japanese port has two 12 cm/45 naval guns in sandbagged pits, five men to a gun, about 5 rounds a minute out to 15,000 m: they open fire inside about 14 km by day (3 km at night), lead you on your course and speed, and walk their splashes onto you, so change course and speed to throw them off. The 25 mm guns at the ports and airfields, and on the escort off the port, fire inside about 2,500 m. Knock the coast guns out with the 3″/50. Hits on the ship are counted.
Shell camSettings: follow your last shells to the target (off at first). Any key returns to the gun.

The torpedoman

OrdersThe OOD's orders come on the phone box: go to the weapon it names (6 or J in game mode, walk in Hardcore), set the depth, and fire on the order.
Hedgehog24 contact-fuzed bombs thrown in a ring ahead: they burst only if one hits the submarine. R reloads.
K-gunsTwo each side, thrown about 60 yards abeam; each crew reloads its gun in about a minute (half in game mode).
RacksTwo stern racks of 8 charges, dropped port and starboard in turn.
Depth and armingEach position has its own pistol depth setting and safety fork (X arms it). On safe, a charge does not go off.
DebriefAfter the run: kill, damaged or missed, with a plot of where every charge went off.

The ship

EnginesW / S move the engine order (the telegraph) through Back Full, Back 2/3, Back 1/3, Stop, Ahead 1/3, 2/3, Standard, Full and Flank; it stays where you leave it. The engine room answers in a second or two and she gathers way slowly: about 15.7 knots full ahead, 6 astern.
RudderA / D put the rudder over; let go and it stays. Away from the bridge the helmsman holds her on her heading.
SeaShe rides the waves. Game mode: Settings > Wave motion can hold her steady at the guns.
Running agroundShe stops if her leading end would be in water shallower than her draft.
SoundHer two diesels follow the engine room's answer, beating against each other; with the blowers, exhaust, screws, wash and wind, mixed for where you are.
Photo modePause menu > Photo mode, or F9: the game freezes and you get a free camera.
Time & weatherSettings > Time of day & weather.
