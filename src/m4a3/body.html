The tank

The Medium Tank M4A3(75)W was the Ford GAA V-8 Sherman with the 75 mm M3 gun and wet ammunition stowage: the main-gun rounds sit in water-jacketed racks under the turret floor. It was the type the 6th Tank Battalion took to Okinawa in 1945. Ours is a late-production tank: the large-hatch hull, the high-bustle turret with the loader's hatch and the commander's all-round vision cupola, VVSS suspension.

Five men crew her: the commander, gunner and loader in the turret, the driver and the assistant driver (the bow gunner) in the hull. Each of the five stations will be playable, with the AI crew manning the rest.

How this game compares with the real tank

Overall: the figures come from the M4A3(75)W late-production spec sheet, TM 9-2800 (1943) and the 75 mm M3 gun data. The groundwork version shows the tank standing; her driving, gunnery and crew come in the next phases.

Phone & tablet

FullscreenThe flight starts fullscreen, which hides the Android status and navigation bars. Swipe in from the screen edge to show them for a moment. The ⛶ button toggles it, and the pause menu can turn the automatic fullscreen off.
ExitTap the pause button, then Exit game. It leaves fullscreen and closes the app. This works even when the phone’s swipe and Back gestures are hidden by fullscreen.
Hold itSideways (landscape) like a steering wheel works best; portrait works too. Keep the screen upright and facing you: leaning it back a long way makes a twist also nudge the roll. The game calibrates to the way you hold the phone when you start; the target button in the top bar calibrates again.
RollTurn the phone like a steering wheel: clockwise rolls right.
PitchTop edge toward you pulls up; top edge away pushes the nose down (settings can invert it).
RudderTwist the phone left or right, as if turning on the spot. The twist fades after a few seconds, so use the two arrow buttons at the bottom for sustained rudder, for example on the takeoff roll.
ThrottleDrag the slider on the left; drag it to the very top or bottom to snap to full or idle.
ButtonsFIRE (hold), rocket, bomb, gear, brake (hold), flaps up and down. The top bar has the mission menu, pause menu, camera, calibrate, tilt settings and fullscreen.
Without tiltDrag on the right half of the screen as a virtual stick (drag down to pull up). Pinch zooms the camera in or out: it always stays directly behind the plane and never rotates. Double-tap resets the zoom. The camera button switches to the cockpit view.
GraphicsPause menu > Graphics: Auto, Low, Medium or High (N cycles them). Auto picks Low or High and adjusts the resolution to keep the frame rate smooth. Low draws fewer, simpler trees and fewer clouds and no shadow; Medium is in between; High draws everything at full resolution, with detailed branching trees (several kinds of oak and conifer, with textured leaves and needles) around you; to see them, fly to the wooded hill about 4.6 km north-west of the start (heading 323). Close to your own aircraft (Medium and High) it also shows engraved panel lines, rivets, gun barrels, a pitot tube, fuel caps and wheel detail. Near the ground (Medium and High) blade tufts and wild flowers grow around you, fading out about 130 m away. Auto shrinks the zone of detailed trees and grass first, then the resolution, if the frame rate drops. The choice is saved.
Install / offlineWhen the game is served from a web address (for example GitHub Pages) the browser can install it as an app (Add to Home Screen or Install) and it then runs full screen and offline.
Hardcore modePause menu > Hardcore: a master switch and a switch for each real-aircraft detail (torque, spins, altitude, engine temperature, fuel, war emergency power, real-aircraft performance, load limits, trim, real flap and gear speeds, engine power limits, wind, turbulence, wet runway, icing). Off by default.
LayoutSwipe any instrument box off the screen to hide it. The pause menu has a checkbox for everything you can show or hide, and Move boxes lets you drag the boxes and buttons where you want them. Your choices are remembered, separately for landscape and portrait.
TipsiPhone and iPad ask permission for motion sensors the first time. If the page is shown inside another page the browser may block the sensors: open it in its own tab. Use Add to Home Screen for a full-screen app.

OPERATIONS

The tank, the camp and what is coming

Camp Buckner (this version)

The tankYour M4A3(75)W stands in the first revetment of the battalion's tank park at Camp Buckner, behind Beach Orange. Look round her from outside: drag with the mouse (or a finger), the wheel zooms. C changes the view.
Stations1 the commander head out in his cupola; 4 the driver head out, 4 again buttoned up at his M6 periscope (it turns with the head and tilts a little), and 4 a third time down in his seat with the laterals, the gear lever and the instrument box. 2 the gunner at his periscope sight (1x; 2 again 6x, again the 3x telescope); 3 the loader at the breech. C goes back to the chase view. The bow gunner comes with the command phase (T4).
DrivingW is the accelerator, S the brakes (stopped, S backs her up), A and D pull the left and right laterals. The gears change themselves: she pulls away in 2nd (1st up a steep slope or in a paddy) and runs up to 5th, about 43 km/h flat out on a road. The laterals turn her on the controlled differential: never on the spot, and a pulled lateral costs speed. She climbs a 60% slope, a 0.6 m step and bridges a 2.3 m ditch; a paddy or sand holds her back; a wall or a building stops her.
The groundRound the camp lies the close country of the south: small fields of cane, sweet potato and vegetables, low paddies, coral-block walls with gaps and gates, fukugi windbreaks and scrub on the slopes.
GunneryAt the gunner (2): the mouse (click) lays the in-game gun where you point, Space fires, F the coaxial machine gun, Z the stabilizer, Q / E the round to call (AP, HE, SMOKE). The reticle's range lines are for the round loaded. The loader loads what is called (the AI, or you at 3: Space takes the round, Space again rams it when the marker is in the green). These are game settings for now; the real figures come later.
Buckner RangeSouth of the camp. At the gunner or the loader, T opens the gunnery drills: stationary boards at 400 to 1,200 yards, a moving board, battle sight, range estimation, the stabilizer (fire on the halt), and loading against the clock. The commander gives each fire command and senses each burst.
Training drillsAt the driver (4), T opens the list: the driving course (time, collisions, stalls), the course buttoned up on the periscope, and hull-down behind the berm south of the course, judged by what a target 290 m away can see of her.
Buckner CourseNorth of the park, marked with boards: a 2 m ditch, a 0.6 m step, a 4 m slope (50% up), a wall with a gap, a paddy and two terrace steps; south of it the hull-down berm.
The campThe park's ten revetments, the maintenance Quonset, the ordnance and fuel dumps, the company CP and the bivouac. Buckner Range is on the rising ground to the south.
